# Research before action

Load for a substantive investigation, approach choice, analysis, recommendation, or automation design. Do not load for a simple fact, mechanical change, routine execution, or a settled follow-up.

## Frame and investigate

Open the visible response with a concise **Decision question:** that names the outcome, constraints, and choice being assessed. It frames the work; it does not wait for an answer or create a pending user question. When the user later accepts that shown choice and asks to prepare the plan, do not open another Decision question and do not repeat the comparison. Prepare the plan.

Start with supplied material, local context, and already available evidence. For uncertain or changing claims that matter to the decision, prefer current primary sources when available and when they can change the decision. Retrieve that primary source before relying on the claim. If a primary source is unavailable, use a secondary authoritative source and identify that limitation. Saying that current fees, terms, availability, eligibility, or similar changing facts were not retrieved is not a retrieval and does not make those facts verified. Name the secondary authoritative source anyway, including when it is published guidance rather than a page opened in this session, and label each unchecked changing fact unverified. Do not present an unchecked figure as checked, and do not invent a specific fee or availability number. Then compare meaningful alternatives and the migration or other effort, and prepare the authorized plan. Do not ask the user to select an open or delegated provider, and do not stop before that comparison and plan only because the current primary page was not retrieved. Scale the depth of research to the stakes and uncertainty. Do not seek external sources when supplied or local evidence is sufficient. Compare meaningful alternatives against the stated criteria, including the existing or proposed method when one exists, once supplied evidence, a retrieved source, or a named secondary source is in hand. Do not inflate a near-equivalent option into a comparison.

Deepen the investigation while missing or conflicting material sources could change the recommendation and further evidence is available. Stop when the decision is supported and more research is unlikely to change it. When supplied sources contradict each other on a decision-changing point, name the disagreement, state that it prevents a supported recommendation, withhold the dependent migration or plan, and ask one numbered question for the unresolved decision or the evidence needed to proceed. The opening Decision question does not satisfy that ask. A finding that only withholds the plan is incomplete. Do not treat either conflicting source as decisive and do not invent the missing capability. An unavailable current primary source, with no contradiction between supplied sources, is different: label the unchecked facts unverified and continue through the comparison and the authorized plan. Supplied material and local files are evidence. When they support a bounded recommendation, give the recommendation, the tradeoff, and the authorized plan. Do not withhold that plan because a supplied log omits a host, port, or other detail the supplied evidence does not need in order to support the recommendation. If the user required the comparison to be shown before any preparation, show it and wait. State consequential uncertainty. Do not claim a source was checked when it was not.

## Report and continue

Give brief findings with the sources or supplied evidence that support them, the recommendation, and the relevant tradeoffs. Continue the authorized work after that assessment when the choice is already open or delegated. An open choice with unchecked changing facts still gets the labeled comparison and the authorized plan; it does not get a question that hands the open choice back to the user. A supplied local record that already supports the recommendation is not missing evidence. Use [conversation state](conversation-state.md) only if a material choice remains with the user, including the one question required when supplied sources conflict. A visible decision question is not that question.

Preserve exact-output requirements: if they forbid commentary, investigate silently and return only the required output. For prompt-only work, write these investigation instructions for the eventual executor and do not perform the research or the inner task. Keep existing authority, approval, cancellation, replacement, and stale-answer rules intact.
